Twin Home Buyer · Google Ads operating system · Build plan for the Claude Code team

Teach Google what a real seller looks like.

We are not building another Google Ads dashboard. We are building the system between our business and Google: one landing page that works, tracking that never lies, lead grading, and a feed of qualified-seller and contract outcomes back to Google so its bidding chases sellers who sign, not form fills.

Click→Landing page→Lead→Qualified→Appointment→Offer→Contract→Closed $→Back to Google
Google runs the auction

Smart Bidding sees auction signals we never will. We don't try to outbid it by hand.

Claude runs the intelligence

Watches spend, grades leads, finds waste, writes the morning brief, flags fires early.

Humans make the calls

Nothing changes in Google Ads without a named person approving it and a logged reason.

House rules

Put these at the top of CLAUDE.md in the repo
  1. Optimize toward qualified sellers, then contracts and profit. Never toward clicks or raw form fills when better data exists.
  2. Never make a meaningful change without recording who, why, and what happened after.
  3. Never let broken tracking teach Google. If tracking breaks, stop trusting the numbers until it's fixed.
  4. Never judge a campaign on immature data. Real estate leads take weeks to become contracts.
  5. Change one variable at a time. Five changes at once teaches nobody anything.
  6. One control landing page. We improve it with tests; we don't throw it out after three days.
  7. Claude observes and recommends. Humans approve anything that spends money or changes targeting.
  8. "No action" is a valid recommendation. Leaving Smart Bidding alone is often the right call.

The build

~20 focused hours · then a week live on real traffic
Start the slow approvals in hour zero. A Google Ads API developer token needs Google's review before it works on the live account, and that can take days. Apply first thing, build against a test account while you wait. Also confirm today, in Google's own current docs, which API accepts offline and enhanced-conversion-for-leads uploads for our account (Google has been moving this to the Data Manager API). Build to whatever that doc says, not to an old tutorial.
